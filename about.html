— About

About me

Engineer by training, designer by practice, engineer again by choice

I studied Mechanical Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO). Engineering taught me how systems work, but I fell in love with how people use them. That led me into product design.

Since then I've designed and managed products across HR tech, AI tools, e-commerce and education. My favourite way to work is design thinking: understand people first, sketch fast, test early. I also teach design, product management and AI automation, and I've led design teams.

Today I'm a Product Manager at TechBridge City in Port Harcourt, and a first-year Software Engineering student at Miva Open University. I'm going back to engineering, this time to software, so I can build the products I imagine.
